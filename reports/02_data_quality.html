Data quality assessment¶

Summary¶

Assessment of source completeness, cancellations, duplicate records, numeric validity, invoice consistency, and temporal coverage. All original records are retained.

Source: Chen, D. (2015). Online Retail, UCI, CC BY 4.0. Input: data/raw/Online Retail.xlsx, sheet Online Retail. Prices are GBP. The source grain is an invoice line item; a unique line identifier is not supplied.

Method and scope¶

Checks cover the complete workbook, including anonymous records, cancellations, and adjustments. Flag percentages use all raw rows unless another denominator is specified. Text normalization is evaluated on a temporary copy; no cleaned transaction dataset is produced.

Completeness¶

Null counts and rates are calculated by column. Blank text is checked separately. Missing customer identifiers limit customer-history analysis but do not independently establish invalid sales. Workbook observations take precedence over conflicting source metadata.

Review flags¶

C-prefixed invoice identifiers are documented as cancellations. Negative quantities can also represent adjustments. Flags overlap and must not be summed as independent defects. A negative quantity alone does not establish a physical return.

Duplicate records and candidate keys¶

Exact copies beyond the first occurrence are distinguished from all members of duplicate groups. A sensitivity check normalizes whitespace and case on a temporary copy. Neither repeated invoice/product pairs nor matching row values establish a unique line key or prove ingestion duplication.

Numeric exceptions and item codes¶

Quantiles and extreme observations identify quantities and unit prices requiring review. Large values can reflect wholesale transactions. Item codes outside the five-digit pattern with up to two letter suffixes are review candidates; format exceptions are not automatically invalidated.

Temporal coverage¶

Monthly missing-customer shares are paired with raw row denominators. December 2011 is partial. Changes in completeness do not establish an ingestion failure or its cause. The source provides no ingestion timestamp or documented timezone.

Reconciliation and source preservation¶

Monthly totals reconcile to source counts. Independent Python recounts verify missing customer IDs and C prefixes. Workbook and DataFrame fingerprints confirm that profiling preserves the source records. These checks validate the calculations, not the commercial legitimacy of every entry.

Observed counts, interpretation limits, and proposed treatments are exported to docs/data_quality.md. Compact evidence summaries are saved under data/processed/; they are not cleaned transaction records.
